# Files

- [Audio Callback and Rendering](audio-callback-and-rendering.md) - The realtime half of AudioPlayer — the lock-free cpal data callback and its Renderer, which plays whole frames from the ring, skips audio below the clear() watermark with a 5 ms fade-out, pads with silence, converts to the device sample type, and publishes the consumed and audible_until values that drain() waits on.
- [Blocking Waits and Stream Failure Handling](blocking-waits-and-stream-failures.md) - How AudioPlayer's blocking write() and drain() wait without the GIL — 5 ms polls, 100 ms signal checks, poll-counted stall detection — and how cpal stream errors are classified, throttled, latched into the player and surfaced as RuntimeError and is_active=False.
- [Interruption, Drain and Stop Semantics](interruption-and-drain.md) - The cross-thread protocol behind clear(), stop() and drain() — monotonic written/consumed sample counters, an epoch and a discard watermark — including the SeqCst pairing that stops a racing write() from replaying cleared audio, when a stream ends and restarts, and the barge-in usage pattern.
- [Resampling and Channel Mapping](resampling-and-channel-mapping.md) - How the stateful Converter turns the caller's sample rate and channel count into the device's — exact integer positions that make Catmull-Rom resampling independent of chunking, priming and reset at stream boundaries, a two-frame look-ahead with no end-of-stream flush, clamping, and positional upmix/downmix rules.
- [Stream Setup and Config Negotiation](stream-setup-and-negotiation.md) - What happens when an AudioPlayer is constructed — argument validation, device lookup, choosing the device stream configuration (negotiate() and the macOS exception), sizing the ring buffer, dispatching on the device sample format, starting the stream, and recording caller versus device format.
- [WAV File Playback (play_file)](wav-file-playback.md) - How pyaudiocast.play_file() streams a WAV file to a device — opening it off the GIL, creating a player at the file's format, decoding and pushing 4096-frame chunks, tolerating truncated or pipe-written files, raising other decode errors only after playing what decoded, and always releasing the device.
- [Write Path and Input Formats](write-path-and-input-formats.md) - How AudioPlayer.write() turns bytes, bytearray, list[float] and numpy arrays into normalized f32 frames — input detection with optional numpy, shape/dtype/alignment/contiguity handling, sanitization, carry-over of half samples and partial frames between writes, and the blocking push loop into the ring buffer.
